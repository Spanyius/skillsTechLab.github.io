Main focus of the lab

Four connected directions

The lab is part of VSB – Technical University of Ostrava, Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, Department of Computer Science. Its main focus combines three research and development pillars with strategic collaboration and networking.

01

Skills Intelligence

Data-driven and AI-driven methodologies, tools for extraction, analysis, matching and forecasting, skills intelligence gathering, and reference definitions of skills and job roles.

View focus
02

Advanced Learning

AI-powered learning approaches, adaptive learning, recommendation systems, personalised educational pathways, digital delivery, VR/XR, digital tools, and micro-credentials.

View focus
03

Technological Innovation

System and software development processes, quality, safety and cybersecurity, AI across the development life cycle, connected and automated driving, and software-defined vehicles.

View focus
04

Strategic Collaboration

A leading role in the European skills sector, collaboration within Union of Skills networks including Pact for Skills, and events at European, national and regional level.
