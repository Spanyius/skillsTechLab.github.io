Focus 01

Technological Innovation

Software & system engineering in the era of AI · process innovation · technological applications

01

System and software process & development

Research and development of system and software engineering processes with a focus on quality, safety, cybersecurity and continuous process improvement. This includes Functional Safety, Cybersecurity, Automotive SPICE and related engineering frameworks, particularly in the automotive and software-intensive systems domains.

02

AI-facilitated system & software development

Research on how AI tools can facilitate and improve activities across the complete system and software development process — from requirements engineering, architecture and design through implementation, testing and verification to validation, deployment, operation and maintenance. We investigate where AI can effectively support engineers, how human and AI activities should interact, and how AI-assisted work can be integrated into established engineering processes.

03

Software process improvement through metamodels

Research and development of software process metamodels that provide a structured representation of development activities, roles, artefacts, competences, methods and their relationships. The metamodel approach enables comparison and mapping of existing development processes, identification of process gaps and opportunities for improvement, and systematic integration of AI-supported activities into software and system engineering workflows.

04

AI-supported engineering education & competence development

We transfer research results directly into education and professional training. Students and practitioners learn how to apply AI tools throughout the software development process — including requirements, design, coding, documentation, testing, verification and process management — while understanding their limitations, quality implications and appropriate role within professional engineering practice.

05

Technological innovations & applications

Research and development of technological applications in areas such as connected and automated driving, software-defined vehicles, cybersecurity, functional safety and dependable software-intensive systems, linking process innovation with practical industrial applications.

Focus 02

Advanced Learning Solutions

Recognition · recommendation systems · educational pathways

01

AI-powered learning approaches

Research and development of adaptive learning, recommendation systems and personalized educational pathways. Our work also uses AI-assisted mapping to propose competence matches from course descriptions and learning outcomes, supporting faster mapping of learning offers to reference job-role and competence frameworks.

02

Innovative delivery of needed skills

Research and development of effective digital learning, including AR/VR/XR, interactive content and gamified learning experiences. We work with European education and training partners, including Chalmers University of Technology in Gothenburg and ATEC.

03

Digital learning ecosystem

We connect course delivery, smart catalogues and competence-aware discovery through the Learning Platform, Skills Hub and Dovednosti-MSK.

04

Recognition solutions & micro-credentials

Research and development of recognition approaches that connect learning outcomes with sector competence references. The Skills Hub supports micro-credentials for mapped courses, making achieved learning and competence evidence more explicit and portable across the ecosystem.

Focus 03

Skills Intelligence Anticipation & Gathering

Methodology · tools · execution

01

Data-driven methodologies

Research and development of data-driven and AI-assisted methodologies that combine desk research, quantitative signals, stakeholder evidence and expert validation to understand how technologies, regulation and industrial transformation affect jobs and skills.

Long-running approach: methods have been iterated across European skills projects including DRIVES, ALBATTS, TRIREME and related sector initiatives.
02

Skills intelligence tools

Research and development of tools for skills extraction, analysis, semantic matching, mapping and forecasting. Skills Hub and Dovednosti-MSK operationalise parts of this work through searchable reference catalogues and links between courses, competences and job roles.

AI mapping: competence candidates can be proposed from course descriptions and learning outcomes, supporting faster course-to-competence mapping before expert/provider validation.
03

Skills intelligence gathering & update

Continuous gathering and update through desk research, surveys, interviews, workshops, labour-market and sector data, stakeholder engagement and expert communities. The work spans automotive-mobility, batteries, chemicals, semiconductors and other changing industrial domains.

04

Reference definitions of skills & job roles

Research and development of sector-specific job-role, competence and knowledge definitions, including ontology/competence-matrix structures, maturity/level concepts and continuous update mechanisms. These definitions connect skills intelligence with training, recruitment, career pathways and recognition.

A continuous skills intelligence cycle

A practical synthesis of the approach repeatedly applied and refined across the lab’s European skills work:

1Scan change.
Desk research, trends, regulation, technologies and labour-market signals.
2Gather evidence.
Surveys, interviews, workshops, stakeholder communities and expert input.
3Analyse needs.
Identify skills gaps, emerging competences, role changes and training implications.
4Define references.
Update job roles, competence matrices, skills/knowledge definitions and Skills Cards.
5Map learning.
Connect courses and learning outcomes with competences and role requirements, increasingly supported by AI-assisted mapping.
6Act & update.
Turn intelligence into strategy, roadmaps, training and recognition — then repeat as the ecosystem changes.
