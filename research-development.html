SkillsTech Lab

Research & Development Focus

The lab develops methodologies, tools, software and system approaches across skills intelligence, advanced learning solutions, and technological innovation.

Focus 01

Skills Intelligence Anticipation & Gathering

Methodology · tools · execution

01

Data-driven methodologies

Research and development of data-driven methodologies, including AI-driven approaches.

Example: work is carried out in EU projects, e.g. leading work packages in ChemSkills.
02

Skills intelligence tools

Research and development of tools for skills intelligence support, e.g. skills extraction, analysis, matching, and forecasting.

Example: BI approach and job advertisements analysis.
03

Skills intelligence gathering & update

Advanced skills intelligence gathering and update via various methods.

Example: gathering in various domains, including automotive, chemical, semi, etc.
04

Reference definitions of skills & job roles

Research and development of reference definitions of skills and job roles: methodology, ontology, definition and update.

Example: work in TRIREME/TRAUTOM for automotive and other sectors.

Focus 02

Advanced Learning Solutions

Recognition · recommendation systems · educational pathways

01

AI-powered learning approaches

Research and development of AI-powered learning approaches, including adaptive learning, recommendation systems, and personalized educational pathways.

02

Innovative delivery of needed skills

Research and development of innovative and effective ways of delivery needed skills, using e.g. digital solutions and VR/XR.

03

Digital solutions for skills & workforce ecosystems

Research and development of advanced digital solutions and tools for skills and workforce ecosystems, such as Skills Hub, Learning Platform, and others.

Skills HubLearning Platform
04

Recognition solutions

Research and development of recognition solutions, including micro-credentials.

Focus 03

Technological Innovation

AI-powered SW/SYS solutions · SW/SYS development process

01

System and software process & development

Research and development of system and software process and development, considering quality, safety and cybersecurity — e.g. for the automotive domain: Functional Safety, Cybersecurity, and Automotive SPICE.

02

AI across the product development life cycle

Research on AI impact in the whole life cycle of product development.

03

Development process in the era of AI

Research on development processes in the era of AI, including metamodels, agile, etc.

04

Technological innovations & applications

Research and development in areas such as connected and automated driving, software defined vehicles, and cybersecurity.
